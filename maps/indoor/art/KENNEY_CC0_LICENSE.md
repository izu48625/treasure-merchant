**Usage scope:** A V5-only visual experiment in the existing STORAGE room. Original
Tiled `Floor`, `Furniture`, `Walls`, `SetDressing`, `InteriorThemes`, collision,
line-of-sight, enemy patrol and loot positions are kept as-is. Optional asset load
failure falls back to the previous art. Visit `?v=R5.80&warehouseArt=0` to disable
the pilot art and compare; default loads the new warehouse appearance.
